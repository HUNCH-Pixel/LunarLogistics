Our project

The game we are building

NASA HUNCH asked for a lunar cargo simulation: pack a container on Earth, unload it on the Moon. [1] This is how we are answering it.

The idea

Replayable, first person, and scored

We sketched the game on a whiteboard in September. It is completely first person and has two phases: Earth and Moon. There is no end goal, so you can replay it. Your score depends on efficiency and time, and the supplies are randomized each run.

Players keep track of what they load with a small tablet that logs where every item was placed. A mission lander talks to you through dialogue, and we may add a rocket ride to the Moon for polish.

Phase 1 · Earth

You pack a long cargo container. We think about it as loading Day 14 supplies first, so the Day 1 supplies are by the door when we land.

Phase 2 · Moon

You unload with a robot helper. The container opens, you pull out what the crew needs first, and the log updates as things leave.

Decisions so far

What we have settled on

We chose a 14-day mission and are planning for a crew of four. Our teacher passed along advice from HUNCH that a door in the middle of the container could cut how far a robot has to travel, and that a moving rack is worth a look. We are testing that idea.

We are aiming to have a couple of basic missions working for the PDR, such as building the 14-day manifest and loading in reverse order, and to save the harder ones for later. [Confirm which missions you are targeting]
