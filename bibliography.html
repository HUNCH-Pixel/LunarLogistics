Where our information comes from

The gold numbers on each page point to these sources. If something on the site is not listed here, it is our own work or still being checked.

  1. NASA HUNCH. “SFT 2026-27 LLASO Project 1 – Lunar Logistics Supply Chain (VR Simulation, Game or Math Model),” reference LLASO-P1-VR-2026. nasahunch.com. Used for: the project brief.
  2. Lynch, C. S., Goodliff, K. E., Stromgren, C., Vega, J., Ewert, M. K. “Logistics Rates and Assumptions for Future Human Spaceflight Missions Beyond LEO.” AIAA ASCEND, 2023. NTRS 20230013896. ntrs.nasa.gov. Used for: consumption rates and the 0.053 m³ single-bag equivalent.
  3. NASA. “SEATEST 6 Detailed Final Report,” NASA/TM-20230013000, 2023. ntrs.nasa.gov. Used for: the 2-crew, 14-day estimate of about 37.5 bag equivalents.
  4. NASA. “NASA Names Winners in Lunar Gateway Packing and Storing Challenge.” nasa.gov.
  5. GrabCAD. “NASA Challenge: Lunar Gateway Cargo Packing and Storing,” results page. grabcad.com.
