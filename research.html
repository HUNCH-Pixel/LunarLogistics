Cargo Transfer Bag Equivalent

CTBE = 0.053 m³

External volume of one single-CTB equivalent, with a cargo volume limit of 0.049 m³ and a cargo mass limit of 26.81 kg. [3]

Benchmark

≈ 37.5 CTBE for 2 crew, 14 days

NASA’s SEATEST 6 pre-test analysis estimated this need, calling it preliminary. At 0.053 m³ each that is roughly 2 m³ (our arithmetic). [5]

Benchmark

436.7 kg total

NASA’s worked example for 2 crew, 14 days, including water, gas and their carriers. [3]

Takeaway: consumables for a short mission fill only a small part of a 40 ft container, so our design must justify how the rest of the volume is used (racks, aisles, hardware, spares, waste and return cargo).
