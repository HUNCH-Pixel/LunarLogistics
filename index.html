Mission at a glance

Two phases, one supply chain

NASA requirement

Phase 1 · Earth

Optimized packing and loading of a cargo module up to 40 ft long (or a 3 m diameter cylinder of equal length). [1]

NASA requirement

Phase 2 · Moon

Arrival and pressurized unloading in 1/6 g, with minimum human involvement, potentially using robots. [1]

NASA requirement

Track everything

Location, size and weight of every item, measured by time, quantity, weight and tracking. [1]

Our approach

Replayable, scored game

First-person play, randomized manifests, and scoring based on efficiency and time. Details are on the Project page.
