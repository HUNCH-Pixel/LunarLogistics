Requirements

What NASA asks for

Container

Cylindrical or box module, about 3 m diameter and 40 ft long, with racks, stacks and other internal infrastructure. [1]

Gravity

1/6 g physics on the Moon and normal gravity on Earth. [1]

Constraints

No regolith in pressurized spaces, a generic launch vehicle, and an interior reachable by robot or human. [1]

Cargo types

CTBs and metal lockers (1U–6U), plus odd items such as PODS and cylinders. [1]

Interactive concept

LIFO loading preview

NASA’s rule: supplies needed on Day 14 load first and Day 1 supplies load last, so the earliest-needed cargo is most accessible at lunar arrival. [1] Click a day to inspect it.

Mission structure

The 18-mission challenge

HUNCH groups the missions into three phases and says to do as many basic missions as possible by PDR, then attempt the innovative ones for CDR. [1]

Tracking

One record per cargo action

HUNCH asks for a complete record, including a “correct actor assignment” for every action. [1]

Item → Day needed → Mission → Location → Destination → Priority → Handling → Actor → Retrieval time → Status
