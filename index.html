Pixel Packers

We are the Pixel Packers! Our goal is to make a first-person simulation game that teaches players how to accurately manage, load, and unload a lunar container on the moon with minimal effort. [1]

Meet the team →   See our project

How our game works

From Earth to the Moon

1 · Pack on Earth

You load crates into the container. What the crew needs last goes in first, and every item gets tracked.

2 · Launch

A rocket takes the container to the Moon. We want a quick fast-travel moment here to keep it polished.

3 · Unload on the Moon

Gravity drops to one sixth and you unload fast, with help from a robot, keeping astronaut effort low.

Where we are

Still learning, and building anyway

Unity, C# and 3D modeling were all new to us, so a lot of our first two sprints went to figuring out the tools while we researched what a 14-day lunar supply run really needs. We have a map of the Earth phase underway and our first box-versus-cylinder results.

The story of what we have learned is on the Research page, and the game plan is on the Project page.
